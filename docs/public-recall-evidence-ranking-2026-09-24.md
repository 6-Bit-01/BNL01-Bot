## Existing readers now rank eligible evidence

- Original Discord messages retain the resolved speaker, public-source,
  guild, role, date and correction controls. Query overlap ranks candidates
  before the existing row limit; zero overlap no longer excludes them.
  Uncertainty about a task or conversational referent does not revoke an
  independently resolved speaker's attributed background; uncertain identity
  candidates still do not authorize a source read.
  A resolved speaker can also receive their own public originals without an
  additional platform/activity-word check.
- Public show episodes and their authored messages retain subject/source
  bindings and episode/date scope. Topic overlap orders candidates, including
  older matches, without removing the resolved person's remaining evidence.
  Caller-authorized subject continuity can supply bounded background without
  special wording; an unbound or absent speaker does not acquire another
  person's history.
- The existing memory reader can supply eligible short, medium and long
  history during ordinary conversation without recall keywords. Named member
  memory remains available as lower-authority background for dated requests;
  dated originals retain their actual date scope.

The shared evidence guidance distinguishes candidate access from what a
candidate supports. Background does not prove a requested topic or date, and
a bounded selection does not prove that something never appeared in the
archive. BNL must interpret the supplied evidence against the current request.
The relevance scorer remains lexical: this change does not introduce semantic
search or guarantee recovery of every paraphrase across an unlimited archive.

## Recorded timestamps remain attached to evidence

Existing live/retained episode, interval, operation and packet projections now
render each recorded event instant as Pacific date/time with PDT/PST alongside
its elapsed offset. Directed Discord utterances and separately attributed BNL
replies keep their own instants. Missing or invalid clock metadata remains
unavailable. The recurring schedule never supplies a replacement timestamp.
